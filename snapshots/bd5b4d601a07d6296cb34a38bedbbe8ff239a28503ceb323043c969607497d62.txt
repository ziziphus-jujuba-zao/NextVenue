What’s Included with Your Registration Package?

In Person & Virtual Access

The In Person & Virtual Access package delivers the most complete way to experience RSNA 2026, combining full in-person participation with virtual access during and beyond the annual meeting.

What’s Included?

 * In-person access to eligible programming and Technical Exhibits at McCormick Place
 * Virtual access to six live-streamed plenary sessions in the Arie Crown Theater
 * Recordings of plenary, education, science and industry sessions through February 26, 2027, noon CT.

Please note: Session recordings will be available to view within three hours of their scheduled start time.

Virtual Access Only

The Virtual Access Only package provides live-streamed and on-demand access to RSNA 2026 sessions.

What’s Included?

 * Virtual access to six live-streamed plenary sessions in the Arie Crown Theater
 * Recordings of the following sessions through February 26, 2027, noon CT:
    * Plenary sessions
    * Education sessions
    * Science sessions
    * Industry sessions

Please note: Session recordings will be available to view within three hours of their scheduled start time.

Technical Exhibits Only

The Technical Exhibits Only package provides full access to the North and South Exhibit Halls at McCormick Place, where industry innovation takes center stage.

What’s Included?

 * In-person access to the Technical Exhibits and industry sessions at McCormick Place from Nov. 29 – Dec. 2
 * Recordings of industry sessions through February 26, 2027, noon CT.

In Person Only

The In Person Only package provides meeting attendance at McCormick Place for non-members.

What’s Included?

 * In-person access to all eligible programming at McCormick Place
 * In-person access to the Technical Exhibits and industry sessions at McCormick Place from Nov. 29 – Dec. 2.
 * Recordings of industry sessions through February 26, 2027, noon CT.
