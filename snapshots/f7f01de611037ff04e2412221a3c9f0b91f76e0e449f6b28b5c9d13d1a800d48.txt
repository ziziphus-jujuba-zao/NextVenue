Flow of online visa application

Video: How to apply for visa on the JAPAN eVISA website (YouTube)
 * Step 1 Verification of necessary visa to visit Japan

   Verify the type of visa necessary to visit Japan, and the documents to submit.

   Tourism (UP to 90days, Remunerated activities are not allowed)
 * Step 2 Application information entry

   Enter the information necessary for the online visa application.

 * Step 3 Notification of examination result

   The results of your visa application will be sent to your registered e-mail address.

 * Step 4 Visa fees payment

   You will receive a notification email on visa fee. Pay the fee online by your credit card.

 * Step 5 eVISA issuance procedure

   Payment required: eVISA will be issued after the payment.
   Free of charge: eVISA will be issued automatically after STEP 3
