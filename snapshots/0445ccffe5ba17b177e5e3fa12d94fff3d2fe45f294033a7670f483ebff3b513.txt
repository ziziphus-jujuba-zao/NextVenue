Call for Symposium Papers

Important Dates

Deadline for Paper Submission
3 May 2026
Firm Deadline!
Notification Date
1 August 2026
Final Paper Upload and Registration Deadline (NEW)
8 September 2026

The 2026 IEEE Global Communications Conference (GLOBECOM) will be held in the vibrant city of Macau, China from 7 to 11 December 2026. Themed “Communications, Innovation, Inspiration, Intelligence”, this flagship conference of IEEE Communications society will feature a comprehensive high-quality technical program including 12 Symposia and 13 SAC Tracks, and a number of Tutorials and Workshops. IEEE GLOBECOM 2026 will also include an attractive Industry Forum & Exhibition Program with Keynotes from prominent research and industry leaders, business and industry panels, and vendor exhibits.
