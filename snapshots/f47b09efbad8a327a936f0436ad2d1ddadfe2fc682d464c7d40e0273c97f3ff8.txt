Frequently Asked Questions

How can I claim CE credits? Can online-only attendees claim CE Credits? add_circle_outline

Registrants, including online-only attendees, can claim CE credits, request MOC points, access certificates, and provide feedback through the  ASCO Meetings App or the Program Guide which will be available closer to the meeting. Please visit the Continuing Education page for more information. All MOC point requests and CE credit claims must be submitted by September 3, 2026.

How can I get a Certificate of Attendance? add_circle_outline

Certificates of Attendance are available for attendees who need a record of their attendance at the Meeting but do not require documentation for continuing education purposes. Certificates of Attendance are immediately available for download online at profile.asco.org under the “My ASCO” tab and the “CE Transcripts & Certificates” tile. Attendees can log in with their ASCO.org login. Paper copies of Certificates of Attendance are not available.

How can I check my login credentials to access content? add_circle_outline

You must know your ASCO username to login. If you forgot your username, you can request it by entering the email address you used to register. If you forgot your registered email address(es), or have issues logging in, please contact Customer Service.

How can I provide feedback about the meeting? add_circle_outline

You can provide feedback about your overall meeting experience via our Meeting Feedback Form. Feedback about individual sessions you attend can be submitted in the ASCO Meetings app using the green “Feedback” button on the bottom of the session page. Your input is critical to the success and planning of future meetings, and we welcome any feedback on your meeting experience.

How can I follow ASCO on social media? add_circle_outline

Follow ASCO on BlueSky, Facebook, Instagram,  LinkedIn, and X.
