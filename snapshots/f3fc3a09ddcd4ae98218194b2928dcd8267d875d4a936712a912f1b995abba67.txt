International attendee registration

Scientists living in less-resourced countries benefit from nearly a 90% registration discount to minimize the financial barrier to attendance.

Learn if you qualify

Visa information

Traveling to the United States often requires obtaining a visa. Once you register, APS will send a letter of invitation for a visa application. It is very important to plan far in advance and review:

 * Visa information for international visitors
 * Letter of invitation information

Press registration

Complimentary registration is available for members of the media. Those eligible for press registration include:

 * Staff journalists working for editorially independent news outlets
 * Freelance science writers and editors
 * Public information officers working for scientific institutions
 * Students and postdocs enrolled in a science writing program

APS uses a one-time credentialing process to verify eligibility for press registration.

Please contact the APS media team with questions about press registration.

Photo release

APS will take photographs and video at the Global Physics Summit. By registering for the meeting and associated events, you agree to allow APS to use photo and/or video footage of you in APS-related publications, materials, websites, or promotions.

Cancellation policy

Cancellations must be received in writing by Apr. 2, 2027. All cancellations will be assessed a $50 cancellation fee regardless of the reason for cancellation. Please email the APS Registration Help Desk with questions.
