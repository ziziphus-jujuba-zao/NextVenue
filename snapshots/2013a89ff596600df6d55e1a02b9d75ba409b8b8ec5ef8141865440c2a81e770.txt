Entry for Congress Participants

If you need a Visa to enter the Republic of Korea, please apply for one as early as possible. Additionally, applicants affected by these procedures are informed of the need for additional screening at the time they submit their applications and are being advised to expect delays.

K-ETA

Nationals eligible for K-ETA, who wish to enter the Republic of Korea without a visa, must obtain K-ETA approval before boarding the flight or ship. Applying for K-ETA after boarding or while already in Korea may result in disadvantages, such as refusal of K-ETA approval or entry restrictions.

K-ETA assessment typically takes 72 hours or less. However, please note that it may take more than 72 hours due to an unexpected increase in applications or other circumstances. Applications will be reviewed in the order they are received, and there is no expedited service to speed up processing for specific applications.

MORE ABOUT K-ETA

Recommendations

To avoid frustration and disappointment:

 * Advance planning by travelers is essential. Carefully review your eligibility for either a K-ETA or a Korean visa before making travel arrangements.
 * Plan to submit your visa application well in advance of your intended departure date. Contact your nearest Republic of Korea embassy or consulate for a current time estimate and recommendations.
 * Visit the embassy or consular section where you plan to apply for your visa. Visit their website to find important information.

Disclaimer

Please note that this information is given in good faith. Entry regulations may change without prior notice. Participants should always refer to official government sources for the most accurate information.

Visa Letters

Official letters of invitation for visa applications will be available to REGISTERED and PAID participants only. These letters do not and cannot commit the Organizing Committee to any financial obligation. Upon successful completion of your online registration and after having made full payment you will be able to download your visa letter.

Visa Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding visa documentation; invitation letters, or congress-related travel support, please contact the ICRA 2027 Secretariat([email protected]) for assistance.
