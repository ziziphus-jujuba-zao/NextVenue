Message from the General and Program Chairs
Welcome to the 18th IEEE/CVF International Conference of Computer Vision (ICCV 2021),
which we all hope will be our last fully virtual conference as the world makes its steps to
recover from the COVID-19 pandemic. ICCV continues to steadily grow as our bi-annual
international community gathering, showcasing the latest impactful works in the vision
community. We are sad that we will not be able to host the conference in Montreal as originally
planned, but are pleased to have the opportunity to bring the community together online.
In this iteration, the conference received 6152 valid submissions, a 40% increase in
submissions compared to ICCV 2019. We assigned these papers to a total of 4216 reviewers.
Of these, 2746 self-reported as experienced (post PhD) and 1462 as student reviewers. In
total, we released 20195 reviews to papers seeking rebuttals. These reviews were managed
by 233 active area chairs who checked the reviews for quality and completeness. 5486 papers
progressed into the discussion phase (11% withdrawn) of which 4550 papers uploaded author
feedback/rebuttal. Each paper received at least three full reviews, and the acceptance
decisions were made within AC pairs in consultation with additional expert ACs as necessary.
Following the best practice of our community, the Program Chairs did not place any restrictions
on acceptance.
In total 1612 papers were accepted for presentation at ICCV, and are included in the
conference proceedings. The acceptance rate of 26% reflects the high standard of ICCV and
is consistent with the rates of past conferences. Out of all accepted papers, 210 were selected
for oral presentations based on AC recommendations. All papers have poster presentations.
Award papers were selected from a pool of 13 papers nominated for the Marr Prize by at least
one AC and two reviewers; final recommendations were made by an external award
committee.
Additionally this year, we introduced a few initiatives to improve the transparency of the
process and reviewing quality in future conferences. First, we actively shared updates about
the reviewing process through the conference’s twitter account. Second, during the review
process we encouraged ACs to add additional reviewers who were better suited to evaluate
papers in terms of biases, fairness, accountability, transparency and ethical aspects of papers
when they felt it was needed. Third, we offered a google calendar that allows adapting
deadlines to authors and reviewers timezones. Third, we collected review ratings from ACs
which we directly shared with all reviewers, thus closing the feedback loop to reviewers.
Recognizing that many other online conferences have had problems with low engagement
during virtual poster presentations, we have put papers into pods consisting of 5 papers.
Authors of each paper will therefore have both a pod teleconference link and an individual
teleconference for discussions.
We would like to thank everyone involved in making ICCV 2021 a reality. This includes the
organizing committee, workshop and tutorial organizers, area chairs, reviewers, authors,
invited speakers, panelists, exhibitors, without whom this meeting would not be possible. We
particularly note the crucial role of Martin Weiss who acted as the program technical chair and
was of immense help to PCs. We also thank Nicole Finn, Liz Ryan, and C to C Events for their
