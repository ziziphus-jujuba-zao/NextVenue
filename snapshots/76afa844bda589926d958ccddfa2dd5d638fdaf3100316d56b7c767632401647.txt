The 46th Conference on Stochastic Processes and their Applications (SPA) will be held in Melbourne, Australia, from the 5th to 9th July 2027.

It is the most prominent international event in 2027 dedicated to the theory and applications of stochastic processes.

SPA conferences are organised annually under the patronage of the Bernoulli Society for Mathematical Statistics and Probability. The last editions took place in Ithaca (2026), Wrocław (2025), Lisbon (2023), Wuhan (2022), Evanston (2019), Gothenburg (2018). The Bernoulli Society is a section of the International Statistical Institute (ISI).

The 2027 edition is hosted by the School of Mathematics at Monash University, as represented by Tim Garoni.

We all look forward to welcoming you to Melbourne!
