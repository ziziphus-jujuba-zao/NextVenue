Message from the General and Program Chairs
Welcome to the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR 2021) -- held
virtually due to the covid-19 pandemic. CVPR continues to be one of the best venues for researchers in our
community to present their most exciting advances in computer vision, pattern recognition, machine
learning, robotics, and artificial intelligence. With paper presentations, tutorials, workshops, exhibitions,
and several social events (many designed specifically for students), this is a week that everyone should
enjoy.
CVPR continued its rapid growth with a record number of 7093 submissions. The program chairs worked
with 280 area chairs and almost 7500 reviewers to select 1661 papers. Each paper received at least 3 full
reviews, and the acceptance decisions were made within virtual AC triplets. Following the best practice of
our community, the program chairs did not place any restrictions on acceptance. The final acceptance rate
is 23.6%, consistent with the high standard of past CVPR conferences.
Out of these 1661 papers, 295 were designated as “orals”, but due to the virtual nature of the conference,
all papers are presented in the same format: a 5-minute pre-recorded video supplemented with a virtual
poster. Given the global nature of a virtual conference, attendees are given the opportunity to interact with
authors either in an asynchronous chat or over a live video chat during their assigned session. Papers were
grouped into 12 live sessions, distributed across multiple time zones, over the four days of the main
conference. This year’s program also features 15 invited speakers with pre-recorded presentations who will
interact with attendees in moderated panel discussions. Per PAMI-TC policy, program chairs did not submit
papers, which allowed them to be free of conflict in the paper review process.
We would like to thank everyone involved in making CVPR 2021 a success. This includes the organizing
committee, workshop and tutorial organizers, area chairs, reviewers, authors, invited speakers, sponsors,
exhibitors, and everyone else without whom this meeting would not be possible. We also thank Nicole Finn,
Liz Ryan, and C to C Events for their organization of the logistics of the conference.
Last but not least, we thank all of you for attending CVPR and making it one of the top venues for computer
vision research in the world. Enjoy CVPR 2021!!
