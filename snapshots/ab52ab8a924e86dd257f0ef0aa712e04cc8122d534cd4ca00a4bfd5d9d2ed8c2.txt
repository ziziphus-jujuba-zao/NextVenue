Abstracts

Abstracts are a cornerstone of the Annual Meeting, with attendees coming from around the world to explore the latest science. Submit your abstract now in all subspecialties and career levels! Abstracts that have previously been presented or published may be included.

Submit your abstract

Abstract guidelines

2027 Annual Meeting

Abstracts information

All abstract submissions are due by 11:59 p.m. CT on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, and must have a status of “successfully submitted” or “successfully submitted pending disclosure” on the online submission site. Accepted abstracts must be presented in person at the 2027 Annual Meeting in Washington, DC.

Submission fees

A non-refundable submission fee will be charged for each abstract submission. The fee is based on the first author’s training and member status.

 * AAN members: $125
 * Nonmembers: $225
 * Interns, junior residents and fellows, PhD candidates, and students (medical or graduate): $25

Please review the abstract guidelines for important information and email Katie Anderson with any additional questions.

Disclosures

All abstract authors must have an up-to-date complete disclosure statement on file by 11:59 p.m. CT on Thursday, November 19, 2026.

Complete disclosure

Notifications

Notification emails will be sent from AAN@mirasmart.com to the first author on the abstract submission at the end of January. Please check your spam or junk folder in case the email is marked as spam by your provider. Contact Katie Anderson if you have not received your notification email by early February. As abstract presenters, first authors will receive an extension code for the Annual Meeting early registration rate.

Accepted abstracts will be released to the public on Thursday, March 18, 2027. All authors must abide by the AAN’s embargo policies. Abstracts accepted to the Annual Meeting can be presented at another conference as long as the abstract has not been selected for an AAN press release.

Presentation

Accepted abstracts must be presented in person at the Annual Meeting, and meeting registration is required. An individual can present a maximum of five abstracts, and no more than two abstracts in platform sessions. The AAN's Science Committee makes the final decision of approval, abstract assignment, and presentation type based on scientific merit and program considerations. Authors will be allowed to designate a new presenter after the notification emails are sent out.

Withdrawals

Withdrawal requests are permitted until April 23, 2027. However, depending on when the request is made, not all withdrawals may be reflected in print or online publications.

Withdrawal requests should be submitted to Katie Anderson and include the abstract title, ID number, presenter’s name, and reason for withdrawal. Authors not notifying AAN staff of withdrawal, notifying AAN staff after April 23, 2027, or not providing a sufficient reason for withdrawal will be subject to sanctions.

Call for Articles: Submission Deadline October 13

Authors submitting an abstract for the 2027 AAN Annual Meeting are encouraged to submit a full Research Article to Neurology® for potential publication before or during the Annual Meeting. Manuscripts will undergo the journal's standard peer review process; submission does not guarantee acceptance for publication. To be eligible for simultaneous publication with the 2027 Annual Meeting, the corresponding abstract must be accepted for presentation.

Please submit your manuscript by October 13, 2026, and indicate “Annual Meeting Abstract Call for Articles” in your cover letter. By submitting a Research Article, authors agree to complete and return any requested revisions by the specified deadlines to be considered for timely publication.

Manuscript and abstract submissions are completely independent processes, and submitting one does not guarantee acceptance of the other.

Key deadlines

 * October 13: Abstract submissions due by 11:59 p.m. CT
 * November 19: All author disclosures due by 11:59 p.m. CT
 * Late January: Abstract notifications sent from AAN@mirasmart.com via email to first authors
 * March 18: Abstracts available to view online
 * April 23: Withdrawal deadline
 * May 1–5: 2027 Annual Meeting in Washington, DC

2027 Annual meeting

Late-breaking Science abstracts

The Late-breaking Science program highlights the most current research being done in the field of neurology. Abstracts qualify for Late-breaking Science by having key aspects of research conducted after the October abstract submission deadline. These abstracts must be new and of sufficient scientific importance to warrant expedited presentation and publication, and contain timely, significant, and innovative content. Case reports are not acceptable submissions and will not be considered.

Late-breaking Science submissions must not have been presented or published elsewhere before the 2027 Annual Meeting. Accepted abstracts must be presented in person at the 2027 Annual Meeting in Washington, DC, and will be under embargo until the start of the first late-breaking session.

Submission fees

A non-refundable submission fee will be charged for each abstract submission. The fee is based on the first author’s training and member status.

 * AAN members: $175
 * Nonmembers: $275
 * Interns, junior residents and fellows, PhD candidates, and students (medical or graduate): $25

Key deadlines

 * December 1: Late-breaking Science submission site opens
 * February 10: Late-breaking Science abstract submissions due by 11:59 p.m. CT
 * Mid-March: Late-breaking Science abstract notifications sent via email to first authors
 * May 1–5: 2027 Annual Meeting in Washington, DC

Email Katie Anderson with any Late-breaking Science abstract questions.
