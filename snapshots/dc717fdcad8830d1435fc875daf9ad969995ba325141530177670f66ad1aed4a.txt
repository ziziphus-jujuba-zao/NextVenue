IECON 2026 | Doha, Qatar

Conference Overview

IEEE IECON is the flagship annual conference of the IEEE Industrial Electronics Society (IES), dedicated to advancing and sharing cutting-edge research, innovative ideas, and emerging developments across a wide spectrum of industrial electronics and related fields.

The conference embraces diverse topics, including (but not limited to): Power Systems, Power Electronics, Renewable Energy, Smart Cities, Smart Grids, Digital Twins, Robotics and Mechatronics, Data Centers, Cybersecurity, Artificial Intelligence for Industrial Processes, and Industry 5.0.

IECON 2026, the 52nd edition of this prestigious series, will be held in hybrid mode, taking place in Doha, Qatar, from 18 to 21 October 2026, while also offering online participation and presentation options for attendees worldwide.

We warmly invite the global community of researchers, practitioners, and industry leaders to join us in Doha or online for IECON 2026.

IECON 2026 • Doha

Host City

The conference will be held in Doha, Qatar, a rapidly emerging global hub that blends tradition with modernity, while also offering online participation options for attendees worldwide. Doha is easily accessible through Hamad International Airport, one of the world’s best-connected and award-winning airports.

With its striking skyline, world-class infrastructure, and remarkable cultural investments, the city exudes confidence and sophistication. Visitors can stroll through Souq Waqif, admire the Museum of Islamic Art, and explore the cultural district of Katara.

From modern malls to traditional souqs, Doha harmoniously balances contemporary life with heritage—an inspiring backdrop for IECON 2026.
