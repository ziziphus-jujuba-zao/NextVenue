IMECE 2026

Join us this November Vancouver, Canada

ASME's International Mechanical Engineering Congress & Exposition (IMECE) is the only event where research leaders from academia, government, and industry converge to make connections and share insights for innovation across engineering disciplines.

This year's IMECE will emphasize the critical role of mechanical engineering in addressing global challenges through innovation and resilience.

Present Your Research or Attend to:

 * Meet your next R&D partner or employer in academia, industry, or government
 * Unlock new research ideas and deepen your expertise across nearly 20 technical tracks
 * Build your résumé and your organization's brand
 * Share your insights with ASME’s largest and most diverse conference community

Don't miss your chance to connect with 2,000+ research leaders, advance your career, and innovate at the intersection of engineering disciplines.
