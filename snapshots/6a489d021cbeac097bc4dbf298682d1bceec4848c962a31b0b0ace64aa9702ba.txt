ITS World Congress 2026 - Gangneung, South Korea

 * Congress

Copyright © Source: https://2026itsworldcongress.org

ERTICO - ITS Europe is working closely with ITS Asia-Pacific and ITS America to co-organise and develop the Congress Programme for the 32nd ITS World Congress in Gangneung, from October 19-23, 2026. This collaboration across regions underscores the event's global nature, giving you direct access to breakthrough innovations, influential decision-makers, and the trends transforming the world’s transport and smart mobility landscape. ERTICO, in particular, facilitates the participation of European stakeholders who aim to present their pioneering solutions during the Congress.

Beyond Mobility, Connected World

The 2026 ITS World Congress will highlight automated driving and electric vehicles, smart infrastructure, mobility services, AI-driven & data-centric systems, policy and governance.

A significant pillar of this leading event is the Technical Programme, including special interest sessions, technical and research paper sessions, and other interactive sessions.

For more details about the Programme and the submission process, please visit the official ITS Asia-Pacific World Congress website here.
