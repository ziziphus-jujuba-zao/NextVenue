About

Physics Sparks Connection

The call for abstracts is open — share your research in person or virtually.

Submit your abstract

The APS Global Physics Summit creates connections at every scale. By uniting 14,000 scientists across all disciplines of physics, it is the largest physics research conference in the world. This meeting reflects the synergy when people come together to share knowledge, collaborate, and advance scientific discovery.

 * Connect with others in your research areas of interest.
 * Explore cross-disciplinary sessions and collaborative events.
 * Unite to advance physics and create a bright future.
