Abstracts

Present your physics research to over 14,000 physicists from around the world at the APS Global Physics Summit 2027.

Submit an abstract

Submit an abstract

Over 10,000 physics research abstracts are submitted each year to the Global Physics Summit, ranging from quantum physics to soft condensed matter and from astrophysics to gravitational waves. Presenters can choose to present in person or virtually via an oral talk or a poster.

You must be an APS member to submit. The call for abstracts runs from Sept. 9 to Oct. 22, 2026.

Review the sorting categories

To submit your abstract, you must select a physics topic or sorting category that best fits your area of research.

Types of abstracts

To further the reach of science, the Global Physics Summit will have in-person and virtual presentation options. APS members who wish to present may submit contributed abstracts for oral or poster presentations.

Individuals may also be invited to speak by the APS Program Committee. Invited speakers must still submit an abstract for their presentation.
