Resilience in a Changing World

AIAA SciTech Forum brings together approximately 6,000 attendees from 46 countries to witness breakthrough science, revolutionary technologies, and generation-after-next capabilities. The 2027 forum theme, Resilience in a Changing World, reflects the growing need for adaptation and innovation in an era of rapid technological and global change, inviting attendees to examine the tools, approaches, and breakthroughs that will strengthen resilience across aerospace, defense, and the broader engineering enterprise.
