Registration is open

The ESMO Congress 2026 will take place from 23 to 27 October 2026 in Madrid, Spain with sessions available also through a dedicated platform.

You will need an ESMO account to register yourself or a group.

Registration includes*:

 * Participation in scientific and educational sessions
 * Opportunities to network with speakers, faculty and peers
 * Access to the poster and e-Poster area
 * Interaction with the industry partners in the exhibition area
 * Participation in the Industry Satellite Symposia
 * Live-streamed sessions and sessions on-demand on the virtual congress platform
 * Access to the meeting resources on OncologyPRO after the event
 * Transportation pass (excluding registrations for students, exhibitors, private meetings, industry satellite, exhibition visitors, filming crews and daily tickets)
 * Live reporting, highlights, critical insights on data presented, as well as expert opinions through News and Insights

*Included services as listed above and access to certain industry areas on the virtual congress platform or within the congress venue might be restricted due to regulations for advertising of pharmaceutical products to non-healthcare-professionals or non-prescribers

Registration fees and deadlines

Individual registration will remain open for the duration of the Congress. The ESMO Congress
Secretariat must receive one completed registration per participant and the corresponding payment in EUR by the given deadlines.

To qualify for the reduced member registration fee, ESMO membership must be active for at least ten (10) working days before the registration payment (In accordance with the Payment & Cancellation Policy, member discounts cannot be applied after the payment has been completed). Please visit the membership page on esmo.org to check eligibility criteria, benefits, and to complete the membership application.

Please send an email to registration@esmo.org to request one of the badges in the "Other registration fees" table above. Kindly note, access to the sessions, virtual congress platform, webcasts and transportation pass are not included.

Cancellation and refund policy

Approved refunds* for individual registrations will be issued if written notice of cancellation is submitted to the ESMO Congress Secretariat by 26 August 2026. After this date, refunds will not be possible. Refunds will be credited to the original payment method used for the purchase.
The information above applies to individual registration only. Tickets purchased by third parties and group leaders (standard group registration, bulk registration and voucher redemption) are non-refundable.

*Refunds are granted only in cases where a registered participant cancels their registration because they are unable to attend the event. Refunds will not be granted in the following situations:

 * A participant activates an ESMO membership after completing payment for their registration.
 * A participant wishes to change the payment method used for the original registration.
 * The request is due to exchange rate fluctuations.
 * A participant requests the application of a different registration category or fee after the registration has been paid.

Travel insurance

Adequate travel protection is highly recommended for all congress participants as it protects travellers from risks such as cancellation, loss of passport or checked baggage and may offer coverage for unexpected medical expenses. Having these risks covered ensures an additional layer of protection against financial loss.

Support

Customer service staff will be available during the ESMO Congress 2026 to provide support and answer any questions you may have.

How it works

 1. Contact groups@esmo.org to request group registration using a voucher code, confirming the total number of individuals to be registered
 2. ESMO sends the code and instructions on how participants can use the voucher to register, to the group leader
 3. Group leaders send the invitations directly to participants, including the voucher code and instructions on how to register themselves individually
 4. Participants login to their existing ESMO account or create an account if they do not already have one. Individuals validate their own personal details and login credentials (i.e. ESMO username, place of work)
 5. Participants follow the normal registration procedure, registering themselves individually using the voucher code when prompted
 6. A few weeks before the beginning of the Congress, the participants will receive the registration confirmation email and instructions on how to access ESMO Congress 2026
 7. Group leaders can download online the confirmation of voucher use
