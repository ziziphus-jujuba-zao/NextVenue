NeurIPS 2026 | Hotel Information

Hotel Information

Official group hotel rates for NeurIPS 2026 attendees and exhibiting sponsors across all three conference locations.

🇦🇺 Sydney, Australia
🇺🇸 Atlanta, GA
🇫🇷 Paris, France
📢 Notice

Official group hotel rates for Sydney & Atlanta must be booked through the official Housing Portal (posted end of August 2026) — not directly with any hotel or third party, which risks losing your rate or booking through an unauthorized site.

Ready to book your room?

Use the official portal below to lock in NeurIPS group rates for your conference location.

Book Sydney Hotels Book Atlanta Hotels Book Paris Hotels
Sydney & Atlanta

Exhibiting Sponsors — Room Blocks (10+ Rooms) & Event Space

If your company requires 10 or more rooms, email Neuripshousing@resiada.com with your room count and arrival/departure dates and site of either Sydney or Atlanta. You'll be added to our housing list, and our team will reach out directly to coordinate. If you also need space for a function, please include those details as well.

Paris

Paris Hotel Information & Booking

The NeurIPS organizing team in Paris has appointed Revolugo as the official housing partner for NeurIPS. Revolugo will provide a wide range of accommodation options at special rates negotiated exclusively for NeurIPS attendees.

Due to high demand during this period, we recommend booking your stay in advance.

Conference Venue

The main conference will take place from 9 to 11 December at the Paris Congress Center, 2 Place de la Porte Maillot, Paris 17, France — Metro: Porte Maillot station (line 1).

Workshops Venues

The workshops will be held at 2 venues on 12 and 13 December:

• Paris Congress Center, 2 Place de la Porte Maillot, Paris 17, France — Metro: Porte Maillot station (line 1)

• Sorbonne University – Jussieu Campus, 4 place Jussieu, 75005 Paris — Metro: Jussieu station (Lines 7 and 10)

For further information or group bookings, please contact reservations@revolugo.com or by phone at +33 1 80 06 62 88.
