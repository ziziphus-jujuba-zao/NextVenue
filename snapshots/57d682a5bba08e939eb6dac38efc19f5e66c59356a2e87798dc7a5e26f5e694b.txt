Abstracts and Sessions

January 26         * Extended deadline for submission of symposium,
                     minisymposium, Dual Perspectives, Storytelling session,
                     Basic-Translational-Clinical Roundtables, Neuroscience
                     Networking Events, Professional Development Workshops,
                     and Ask Anything session proposals by 5 p.m. EST
May 27             * Online abstract submission opens
June 11            * Extended deadline for abstract submission, 2 p.m. EDT
June 12            * Edits to submitted abstracts must be entered by 2 p.m.
                     EDT
Early August       * Notifications of abstract acceptance status sent to
                     presenting authors
September 8        * Late-breaking abstract submission opens
September 10       * Deadline for abstract presenters to switch from
                     virtual-only to in-person presentation
September 15       * Late-breaking abstract submission closes
Late September     * Neuroscience Meeting Planner (NMP) available on SfN.org
Late September     * Notification of late-breaking abstract acceptance status
                     sent to presenting authors
Mid-October        * Meeting mobile app available
November 5         * Deadline for abstract presenters to switch from in-person
                     to virtual-only presentation
November 14-18     * Neuroscience 2026 meeting dates

                         companies must submit their applications, with 50%
