Venue

The venue at IJCNN 2027 sets the stage for presentations, networking, and events, shaping the experience and providing space for attendees to connect and learn.

Cape Town International Convention Centre

1 Lower Long Street
Cape Town,
South Africa

Venue Website (external link)

The Cape Town International Convention Centre (CTICC) will serve as the host venue for the International Joint Conference on Neural Networks (IJCNN) 2027, marking a significant return to South Africa for this premier AI and neural systems event. Located on the city’s scenic foreshore with Table Mountain as its backdrop, the CTICC is a world-class facility uniquely suited for a large-scale academic conference. It offers two state-of-the-art auditoriums with superior acoustics for keynote addresses, over 11,000 m² of flexible exhibition space for poster sessions and industry displays, and numerous breakout rooms equipped with high-end audiovisual technology for specialized workshops. Its commitment to sustainability and proximity to the V&A Waterfront ensures that IJCNN 2027 delegates will enjoy both a professional, high-tech environment and easy access to Cape Town’s cultural and natural landmarks.
