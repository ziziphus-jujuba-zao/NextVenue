7–11 December 2026 | San Francisco, CA

Welcome to AGU26

AGU’s annual meeting, the largest gathering of Earth and space scientists, convenes 25,000+ attendees from 100+ countries to share research, connect, collaborate, and inspire one another.

Registration and housing are now open!

Register and Book Housing

Why Attend?

 * Cutting-Edge Science: Discover the latest research in Earth and space sciences.
 * Global Collaboration: Network with experts, policymakers, and emerging leaders.
 * Interactive Sessions: Attend workshops, panel discussions, and hands-on learning experiences.
 * Career Advancement: Access mentoring programs, career fairs, and funding opportunities.

Important Dates & Deadlines

 * 25 August – Attendee, press, and exhibitor registration & housing opens.
 * Early-September – Sessions are scheduled, and the program schedule is created.
 * 29 September – Late-breaking abstract submission deadline.
 * Early-October – Abstract acceptance notifications sent to authors. Scientific Program goes live.
