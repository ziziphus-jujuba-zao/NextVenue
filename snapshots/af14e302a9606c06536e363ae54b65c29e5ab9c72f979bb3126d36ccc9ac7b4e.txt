Venue

Mercado Livre Arena Pacaembu is a cultural and sports Complex located in a football stadium (Pacaembu) in the heart of São Paulo. The Pacaembu Stadium hosted its first matches in 1940, and since then many important events took place there, such as 6 matches in the Football World Cup in 1950, and the last official match of Pelé, the greatest Football player of all time. The Mercado Livre Arena Pacaembu Complex also hosts the Football Museum, as well as different sport facilities such as tennis courts and swimming pools.

The Conference Hall where Interspeech 2027 will take place has 4500 m2 of free area, and 3000 m2 of support area, as well as air conditioning, Wi-Fi and escalators.
