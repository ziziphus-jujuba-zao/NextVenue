General Chairs’ Welcome
It is our great pleasure to welcome you to the 29th ACM SIGKDD Conference on Knowledge Discovery
and Data Mining — KDD’2023. This year’s conference continues its tradition of being the premier forum
for presentation of research results and experience reports on leading edge issues of knowledge
discovery, data science, and machine learning. The mission of the conference is to provide the premier
forum for advancement, education, and adoption of the “science” of knowledge discovery and machine
learning from all types of data; to share novel methodologies that fulfill the needs of heterogeneous
applications and environments and identify new directions for future research and development. These
ideas have the potential to shape and impact our society and environment, and are becoming
particularly important with the emergence of AI in all fields. KDD provides researchers and
practitioners a unique opportunity to share their perspectives with others interested in various aspects
of data science and machine learning.
KDD’23 has a program of three keynotes, 313 research track papers, 184 ADS (Applied Data Science)
track papers, 34 workshops, 33 tutorials, nine special days, three panels, and eight ADS invited talks.
For the first time, we switched to OpenReview with the mission to further improve the review quality
and facilitate the interaction between reviewers and authors. We have introduced several new special
days, such as Large Language Model (LLM) Day, Finance Day, AI for Open Society Day, Entertainment,
Sports, and Media (ESM) Day, Southern California Data Science; and several new panels, such as AI for
Science and LLMs for education & research. The rise of LLMs has been historic and the nature of
creativity itself may change. With this in mind, we have emphasized LLMs in our keynotes, special
days, and panels. Only time will tell whether we went too far or not far enough!
Putting together KDD’23 was a team effort. We first thank the authors for providing the content of the
program. We thank our program committee chairs (Research: Leman Akoglu, Dimitrios Gunopulos,
Xifeng Yan; ADS: Ravi Kumar, Fatma Ozcan, Jieping Ye) for revolutionizing the reviewing system with
OpenReview and our dedicated senior PCs and PCs for their efforts in providing high-quality and
timely reviews. We are grateful to our 58 organizing committee members who worked very hard at
countless tasks: reviewing papers and workshop/tutorial proposals, inviting speakers, organizing
special days/panels, selecting best papers, selecting travel awards, handling local arrangements,
organizing posters, proceedings, undergraduate and PhD consortiums and the KDD cup, publicizing the
conference on social media and the Internet, budgeting, and handling registrations. Special shoutouts
to the sponsorship committee who raised sponsorships tirelessly to support the conference. Finally, we
thank ACM’s SIGKDD (especially Wei Wang’s leadership), Executivents, and our generous sponsors.
We encourage attendees to attend the keynotes and invited ADS talks. These valuable and insightful
talks can and will guide us to a better understanding of the future. Our keynotes include: Ed H. Chi
(Google), Eric Horvitz (Microsoft), and Mihaela van der Scharr (University of Cambridge). The ADS
invited talks are on exciting topics such as healthcare, finance, large language models, and causal
inference. We hope that you will find this program interesting and thought-provoking and that the
conference will provide you with a valuable opportunity to share ideas with other researchers and
practitioners from institutions around the world.

PC Chairs’ Welcome
It is our great pleasure to welcome you to the 2023 ACM SIGKDD International Conference on
Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining in Long Beach, CA. This year’s conference continues its
tradition of being the premier forum for presentation of research results and experience reports
on leading issues in Data Mining as well as in Data Science and Machine Learning. The large
attendance of the KDD conference, and the high impact of the KDD publications, give researchers
and practitioners a unique opportunity to share novel perspectives.
The call for papers attracted submissions from Asia, the Americas, Australia, Europe and Africa.
We received 1416 papers in the Research Track and 725 papers in the ADS (Applied Data Science)
Track and we accepted 313 and 184 respectively.
This year the KDD conference initiated the move of the management of the reviewing process
over to the OpenReview system. The goal of this switch is to further enhance the quality of the
KDD reviewing, by facilitating a stronger engagement of the reviewers and by fostering deeper
discussions among the reviewers and between authors and reviewers. The first use of the system
was very encouraging. Although the look from the outside looks similar to the previous years –
in this first year of using the OpenReview system the discussions have not been made public –
several submissions were discussed in depth using an easy to understand format. We expect that
the KDD community will embrace this reviewing paradigm in the future and make the entire
reviewing discussions open.
The PC chairs would like to thank the Research Track Workflow Chairs, Yu Su, Ziniu Hu, and Yue
Zhao, and the Applied Data Science Track Workflow Chair, Lulu Zhang, for going above and
beyond the call of duty in making sure the transition to the new, easy to use but awkward to
manage reviewing system as smooth as possible. We would also like to extend our thanks to the
Senior Program Committee members and the Program Committee members (147 and 1411
respectively for the Research Track; 50 and 515 respectively for the ADS track) for their very hard
and conscientious work. It takes a lot of effort to produce a technical program on the order of
KDD, but none is as important as the effort of the reviewers. Their work is reflected in a very
interesting and strong technical program. 30 Research Track PC members and 6 ADS Track PC
members were chosen for the Best Reviewer Award in recognition of their work. We would also
like to thank the entire KDD23 organizing committee, and especially the General Chairs, Ambuj
Singh and Yizhou Sun, and the Chair of the ACM Special Interest Group on Knowledge Discovery
in Data (SIGKDD), Wei Wang, for their leadership and confidence.
We hope that you will find this year’s KDD program interesting and thought-provoking. As KDD
remains in the forefront of the intersection of data analysis, data mining, and machine learning,
an area of ever increasing importance, we hope that this year’s conference will provide you with
a valuable opportunity to share ideas with other researchers and practitioners from institutions
around the world.
