WHO ARE WE

The North Amer­i­can Catal­y­sis Soci­ety was found­ed in 1956 to pro­mote and encour­age the growth and devel­op­ment of the sci­ence of catal­y­sis and those sci­en­tif­ic dis­ci­plines ancil­lary there­to; to pro­vide edu­ca­tion­al ser­vices to mem­bers and oth­er inter­est­ed indi­vid­u­als; to orga­nize and par­tic­i­pate in pro­fes­sion­al meet­ings of sci­en­tists; to report, dis­cuss and exchange infor­ma­tion and view­points in the field of catal­y­sis; to serve as a cen­tral exchange for the sev­er­al catal­y­sis clubs con­cern­ing infor­ma­tion on their activ­i­ties; and to pro­vide liai­son with for­eign catal­y­sis soci­eties, with the Inter­na­tion­al Con­gress on Catal­y­sis, and with oth­er sci­en­tif­ic orga­ni­za­tions and individuals.
