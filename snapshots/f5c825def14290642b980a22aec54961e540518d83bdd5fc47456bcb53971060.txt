A fully merged conference

The International Conference on Hybrid Systems: Computation and Control (HSCC) and the ACM/IEEE International Conference on Cyber-Physical Systems (ICCPS) shared a unified program and program committee in 2026 while retaining their individual identities and proceedings.

In 2027, that transition becomes the ACM/IEEE International Conference on Cyber-Physical and Autonomous Systems (HSCC+ICCPS): one conference, one coordinated program committee, and ACM proceedings for Regular, Short, and Tool papers across three connected tracks.

HSCC 2027 and ICCPS 2027 carry forward the foundations and systems communities of both conferences while giving AI-enabled autonomy a full place in the program.

Three coordinated tracks

One program, broad coverage

01

Foundations

Fundamental methods for modeling, analysis, design, verification, and control of cyber-physical and hybrid systems, including rigorous theory, algorithms, and computational tools.

02

Systems and Applications

Architectures, implementation, integration, experimental evaluation, and deployment of cyber-physical systems, including generalizable lessons from real applications.

03

AI-enabled Autonomy

Advances in artificial intelligence, machine learning, robotics, vision, control, and formal methods for autonomous systems that sense, decide, and act in the physical world, together with credible evidence of safety, robustness, and responsible operation.
