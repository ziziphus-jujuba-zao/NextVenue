Visa Information

© Matej Meza

Visa-free entry

EU nationals and citizens of Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway or Switzerland do not normally need a visa to enter Germany and may enter the country with their national identity card provided they do not intend to stay longer than 3 months. Additionally, nationals of Australia, Canada, Isreal, New Zealand, Republic of Korea, or the USA do not need a visa to enter Germany provided they do not intend to stay longer than 3 months.

Schengen Visa

An overview of the countries whose nationals require a Schengen visa for short stays or are exempt from the visa requirement for short stays can be found here and the Visa-Navigator here.

In case that you need a visa to visit the conference, make sure that you familiarize yourself with the processing times. For more information visit the website of the German missions abroad.

Please find a summary of the visa requirements for non-EU citizens for a short stay (up to 90 days):

 * Completed visa application form
 * Valid passport
 * Biometric passport photo
 * Proof of health insurance (valid in the Schengen area)
 * Travel insurance with coverage of min. €30.000
 * Proof of intention to return
   (e.g. employment contract, certificate of enrollment)
 * Proof of financial means (e.g. pay slip, certificate of enrollment)
 * Flight reservation (outbound and return) and proof of accommodation
 * Invitation letter to the conference
   (will be provided with the registration)

You can apply for your Schengen Visa here.

More information on the Schengen visa is available on the website of the Federal Foreign Office here.

After downloading the letter of invitation for Schengen visa from the registration system, feel free to contact visa-helpdesk@2026.ijcai.org in case of questions.
