About

RTAS is a top-tier conference with a focus on systems with timing requirements. RTAS'27 welcomes papers describing applications, case studies, methodologies, tools, algorithms, or hardware/software innovations that contribute to the design, implementation, verification, and evolution of time-critical systems.

RTAS'27 consists of two tracks:

 * Track 1: Systems and Applications
 * Track 2: Applied Methodologies and Foundations
