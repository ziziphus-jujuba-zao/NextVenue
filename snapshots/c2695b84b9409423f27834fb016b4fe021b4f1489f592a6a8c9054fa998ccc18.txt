Key Dates

September 2026

 * September 15, 2026: Early Access to Housing Reservations Opens for Members
 * September 22, 2026: Housing Reservations Open for Nonmember Attendees
 * September 23, 2026: Application Deadline: NextGen Stars
 * September 28, 2026: Regular/First Abstract Submission Opens
 * September 28, 2026: Award Applications Open:
   * AACR Scholar-in-Training Awards
   * AACR Global Scholar-in-Training Awards
   * AACR-Amgen Global Scholar-in-Training Awards
   * AACR Patient Advocates in Cancer Research Awards
   * AACR-Women in Cancer Research (WICR) Scholar Awards
   * AACR Early-Career Scholar in the Field of Cancer Research Awards
   * AACR Faculty Scholar in the Field of Cancer Research Awards
   * AACR Undergraduate Scholar Awards

October 2026

 * October 15, 2026: Abstract Submission Deadline for AACR New Drugs on the Horizon Sessions
 * October 20, 2026: Meeting Registration Opens

November 2026

 * November 10, 2026: Regular Abstract Submission Deadline (11:59 p.m. ET)
 * November 10, 2026: Award Application Deadlines:
   * AACR Scholar-in-Training Awards
   * AACR Global Scholar-in-Training Awards
   * AACR-Amgen Global Scholar-in-Training Awards
   * AACR Patient Advocates in Cancer Research Awards
   * AACR-Women in Cancer Research (WICR) Scholar Awards
 *   November 24, 2026: Award Application Deadlines:
   * AACR Early-Career Scholar in the Field of Cancer Research Awards
   * AACR Faculty Scholar in the Field of Cancer Research Awards

December 2026

 * December 2, 2026: Late-Breaking and Clinical Trials Abstract Submission Opens
 * December 4, 2026: Application Deadline: AACR Undergraduate Scholar Awards
 * December 7, 2026: Early Registration Deadline (lowest rates)
 * December 7, 2026: Submission Deadline for AACR June L. Biedler Prize for Cancer Journalism
 * December 21, 2026: Regular Abstract Status Notifications Sent

January 2027

 * January 6, 2027: Late-Breaking and Clinical Trials Abstract Submission Deadline (11:59 p.m. ET)
 * January 11, 2027: Manuscript Submission Deadline for Simultaneous Publication Consideration (Regular Abstracts)
 * January 21, 2027: Regular Abstract Scheduling Notifications Sent
 * January 28, 2027: Submission Deadline: Final Data for Clinical Trial Placeholder Abstracts

February 2027

 * February 3, 2027: Late-Breaking and Clinical Trials Abstract Notifications Sent
 * February 4, 2027: Online Itinerary Planner Available (tentative)
 * February 4, 2027: Regular Abstract Withdrawal Deadline
 * February 9, 2027: Manuscript Submission Deadline for Simultaneous Publication Consideration (Late-Breaking and Clinical Trials Abstracts)
 * February 12, 2027: Advance Registration Deadline (discounted rates)
 * February 19, 2027: Housing Reservation Deadline
 * February 19, 2027: Cancellation Deadline for Registration Refunds (less $125 processing fee)
 * February 25, 2027: Late-Breaking and Clinical Trials Abstract Withdrawal Deadline

March 2027

 * March 2, 2027: Regular Abstract Titles and Texts Posted to Online Itinerary Planner (4:30 p.m. ET)
 * March 2, 2027: Late-Breaking and Clinical Trials Abstract Titles Posted to Online Itinerary Planner (4:30 p.m. ET); Abstract Texts Embargoed until April 2, 2027
 * March 2, 2027: Abstract Submission Deadlines:
   * AACR Undergraduate Student Caucus and Poster Competition
   * AACR Special Program for High School Students
 * March 5, 2027: Membership Application Deadline to Register for the Annual Meeting at the Member Rate Onsite
 * March 8, 2027: Registration Deadlines:
   * AACR Undergraduate Student Caucus and Poster Competition
   * AACR Special Program for High School Students
 * March 15, 2027: Annual Meeting App Available for Download (tentative)
 * March 19, 2027: Regular Abstracts Published in an Online-only Supplement to Cancer Research

April 2027

 * April 1, 2027: Virtual Meeting Site Available (livestreaming begins April 2)
 * April 2, 2027: Onsite Registration Opens at the Orange County Convention Center (12 p.m. ET)
 * April 2, 2027: Late-Breaking and Clinical Trials Abstract Texts Posted to Online Itinerary Planner and Meeting App (12 p.m. ET) and Published in an Online-only Supplement to Cancer Research
 * April 2, 2027: E-Posters Posted to the Virtual Meeting Platform
 * April 2, 2027: AACR Annual Meeting 2027 Begins (3 p.m. ET)
