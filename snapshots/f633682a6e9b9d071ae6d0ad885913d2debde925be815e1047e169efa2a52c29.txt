Since 1994, across three continents

The 33rd ITS World Congress

Thirty-three editions across three continents, from Paris in 1994 to Birmingham in 2027. Each Congress gathers the global intelligent transport community to share what works, turning three decades of ideas into the systems that keep the world's cities and roads moving.

The host region

UK: A Global Transport Leader

The United Kingdom is one of the world's leading centres for intelligent transport, where government, industry and academia develop, test and deploy new mobility at scale. From connected and automated vehicles to digital infrastructure and cleaner public transport, delegates see real-world deployment, not just hear about it.

Testimonials

What are people saying about ITS World Congress?

Jurgis Vilčinskas, Deputy Head of Delegation, European Union Delegation to Türkiye

Events like the ITS European Congress are key because they turn policy into implementation. They bring together public authorities, industry and financiers to align standards and regulations, accelerate deployment of smart and green mobility solutions and generate concrete, investable projects.

Jurgis Vilčinskas

Deputy Head of Delegation

European Union Delegation to Türkiye

Ms. Hanne Nettum Breivic, CEO, ITS Norway

In my view, the key contribution of events like the ITS Congresses lies in their unique ability to bring the entire mobility ecosystem together to accelerate real-world progress. These Congresses create a rare meeting point where policymakers, industry leaders, researchers, technology developers and city representatives can align on priorities and collectively shape the future of transport.

Ms. Hanne Nettum Breivic

CEO

ITS Norway

Dr. Axel Volkery Head of Unit Research and Innovation, DG Move, European Commission

These Congresses have been very important in two ways: First, they have helped us understand the needs of the sector when we were in the process of thinking and preparing for the revision of the directive. And now they are of course helping us to communicate with the sector on the changes we made and how to implement them.

Dr Axel Volkery

Head of Unit

Research and Innovation, DG MOVE, European Commission

Darren CapesHead of Road Infrastructure Technology, Department for Transport

Paula ClaytonsmithChief Executive, Local Council Roads Innovation Group

Max SugarmanChief Executive, ITS UK
