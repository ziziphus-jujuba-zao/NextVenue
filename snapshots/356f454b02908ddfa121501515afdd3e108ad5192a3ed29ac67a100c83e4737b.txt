Discover the dates and location of future RSNA annual meetings and browse important materials and highlights from recent conferences.

Future Meeting Dates

Each year, the RSNA Scientific Assembly and Annual Meeting is held at McCormick Place in Chicago, Illinois. Below is the schedule of future meeting dates through 2032.

 * 2026: Nov. 29 to Dec. 3 Technical Exhibits close Dec. 2
 * *2027: Nov. 14 to 18 Technical Exhibits close Nov. 17
 * 2028: Nov. 26 to 30 Technical Exhibits close Nov. 29
 * *2029: Nov. 11 to 15 Technical Exhibits close Nov. 14
 * 2030: Dec. 1 to 5 Technical Exhibits close Dec. 4
 * *2031: Nov. 16 to 20 Technical Exhibits close Nov. 19
 * 2032: Nov. 28 to Dec. 2 Technical Exhibits close Dec. 1

*Earlier schedule. The RSNA annual meeting will be held the week prior to the U.S. Thanksgiving holiday.
