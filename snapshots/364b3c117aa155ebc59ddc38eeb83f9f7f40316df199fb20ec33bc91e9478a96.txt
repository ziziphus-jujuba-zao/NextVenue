Welcome to CoRL 2024!

The Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL) is an annual international conference focusing on the intersection of robotics and machine learning. CoRL 2024 will be held in Munich, Germany, from November 6 to 9, 2024.

Everything that you need to know about CoRL 2024 can be found in the attending and contribution section.
