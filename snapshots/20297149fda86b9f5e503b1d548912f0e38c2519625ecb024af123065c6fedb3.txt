exceptional work – Yu, Yue, Ziniu!
2. We did not leverage OpenReview fully since the reviews are not public
this year.
3. Nevertheless, used OpenReview essentially as a proof of concept this
year:
○ Managing a huge workload using OpenReview is feasible.
○ OpenReview does facilitate more interaction between authors and
reviewers.
