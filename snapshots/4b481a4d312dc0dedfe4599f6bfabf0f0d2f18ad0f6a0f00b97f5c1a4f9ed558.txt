Call For Papers

Building on a strong legacy of scientific excellence, the 2027 conference will focus on the theme “Translating Healthcare Innovation: Bridging Labs with Industries” highlighting how breakthroughs in engineering, AI, and biomedical science can be transformed into scalable solutions that benefit patients, providers, and health systems. At this world’s largest international biomedical engineering conference, a broad spectrum of tracks will cover cutting-edge research in biomedical devices, digital health, AI and data science, imaging, biosensors, robotics, and health informatics, alongside sessions dedicated to translation, regulation, and entrepreneurship.

Under this theme, the conference will bring together researchers, engineers, clinicians, policymakers, and industry partners to accelerate the journey from prototype to practice. We particularly welcome contributions that showcase successful academic–industry collaborations, translational pipelines, real-world deployments, and regulatory or reimbursement pathways for innovative technologies. In addition to distinguished keynotes, the program will feature workshops, mini-symposia, special sessions, oral and poster presentations, industry showcases, and events tailored for students, early-career investigators, and startups. We invite you to share your latest advances and join a global community committed to turning lab discoveries into impactful healthcare innovations.

 * Biomedical Signal Processing
 * Biomedical Imaging and Image Processing
 * Bionanotechnology and BioMEMS
 * Cardiopulmonary Systems and Physiology-based Engineering
 * Neural and Rehabilitation Engineering
 * Wearable Biomedical Sensors and Systems
 * Biorobotics and Biomechanics
 * Therapeutic & Diagnostic Systems and Technologies
 * Biomedical & Health Informatics
 * Biomedical Engineering Education and Society
 * Biomedical Engineering Translation

IMPORTANT CHANGE: Starting EMBC 2027, full contribution papers will follow a 5-page limit, where up to 4 pages can contain technical content, figures, and tables, and the 5th page may contain only references (maximum of 20 references).

 * Formatting

    * All papers must use the provided templates to generate your paper.
    * All papers must be written in English.
    * All papers must be original, unpublished work.
    * Each paper may be written by up to 35 authors maximum.
    * All authors must be listed in the paper management system.
    * One author must be designated as the corresponding author for the paper.

   Templates can be downloaded in the paper management system:

    1. Click here for the full papers template (4-5 pages)
    2. Click here for the 1-page Abstract template

    * Each submission will be reviewed for compliance of margins, headers, and footers, page numbers, and other size specifications.  Papers which are non-compliant may not be included in the proceeding.
