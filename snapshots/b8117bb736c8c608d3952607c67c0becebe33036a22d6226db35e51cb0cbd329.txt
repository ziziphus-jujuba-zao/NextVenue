Registration Instructions (PLEASE READ CAREFULLY)

 * This is the the official NeurIPS registration page, we do not work with any booking agencies
 * Registration Cancellation Policy
 * Registration Transfer Policy.
 * Badge Replacement: Treat your badge as cash see the Badge Replacement Policy.
 * Any Physical Registration: Includes the Virtual Pass
 * Affinity, Social, Expo Events: To attend an Affinity, Social or Expo event you need to be registered for the day that the event is held on.
 * Virtual Pass: Includes virtual-only access to the live streams and videos of all the Conference Sessions, Tutorials, EXPOs and Workshops and the ability to interact with attendees.

 * Update your Profile with your Institution
 * Password must be at least 10 characters long.
 * Visa: If you need a visa visit the Visa Information page.
 * Recruitment: If you are open to being recruited by a sponsor, see section 6.
 * Student ID: is required to get student discount. See more.
 * Registration Receipt: email yourself a copy in section 3.
 * Agree to the code of conduct, and privacy policy.
 * Minors: You must be 14 years of age or older to attend in-person and you must attend with an adult
 * Childcare: is available for children 4 months of age to 13 years old.
 * If a session is sold out, you may put yourself on the Waitlist
