26 papers have been accepted at ICML 2021

May 21, 2021 15:49

Update: May 21, June 7, July 21

26 papers have been accepted at the International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML) 2021, a major conference on Artificial Intelligence (July 18-24, 2021, Online). For more details, please refer to the link below.

[Website] https://icml.cc/Conferences/2021

[Accepted Papers] https://icml.cc/Conferences/2021/AcceptedPapersInitial

RIKEN AIP is the No.1 in Japan.
RIKEN AIP has had 26 papers* accepted at the ICML 2021 which is the highest in Japan.
(There are 1184 accepted papers from 5513 submissions, leading to a 21.4 percent acceptance rate)
Also, Director of AIP center, Masashi Sugiyama who has had 14 papers* accepted which is the highest in the conference.

*As of July 20, 2021 according to a survey by RIKEN AIP.
