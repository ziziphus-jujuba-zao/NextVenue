PC Chairs’ Welcome
The 2025 ACM SIGKDD International Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining will be held
in Toronto, Canada from August 3 - 7, 2025. This year’s conference continues its tradition of being the
premier forum for presentation of research results and experience reports on leading issues in Data Mining
as well as in Data Science and Machine Learning. The large attendance of the KDD conference, and the high
impact of the KDD publications, give researchers and practitioners a unique opportunity to share novel
perspectives.
Process Synopsis
For the first time in KDD history, submissions to KDD 2025 were received and processed in two cycles. The
first cycle has abstract and paper deadlines in August 2024. This Volume 1 of the proceedings comprises
papers accepted in the first cycle. In the future, Volume 2 of the proceedings will comprise papers accepted
in the second cycle, with abstract and paper deadlines in February 2025.
The August 2024 call for papers attracted submissions from Asia, the Americas, Australia, Europe, and
Africa. We received 967 submissions in the Research Track and 316 submissions in the ADS (Applied Data
Science) Track. Some of the papers were resubmissions from papers initially given Resubmit decision in
KDD 2024.
For the Research Track, on average, a fresh submission received 5 reviews, while a resubmission received 4
reviews with some of them being repeat reviewers from the original submission in the previous cycle. All
papers received a minimum of 3 reviews. After reviews, authors had a week to post a rebuttal to each review,
followed by a back-and-forth discussion for another week. Each paper received an average of 10 comments.
Following the discussion, a meta-review with an accompanying recommendation was provided by an Area
Chair, overseen by a Senior Area Chair. Papers were carefully discussed among the program committee for
challenging cases. The decisions were made by Program Chairs.
For KDD 2025 August Cycle, the Research Track has an acceptance rate of 19% for all papers, and 43%
considering only resubmission papers. The ADS Track has an acceptance rate of 20% for all papers, and 52%
considering only resubmission papers.
Program Committee
We extend our thanks to the SACs/ACs and the Program Committee members (10 SACs, 79 ACs, and 1269
reviewers for the Research Track; 35 ACs and 267 reviewers for the ADS track) for their conscientious effort
towards producing a high-quality technical program. For the research track, a reviewer was assigned up to
5 reviews, each AC was assigned up to 12 papers, and each SAC was assigned to work with up to 8 ACs.
In particular, we are especially grateful to the reviewers, who expend their time and expertise in not only
evaluating the submissions but also providing feedback to the authors. In recognition of the timeliness of
review submission, quality of the reviews and active participation during rebuttals, we list reviewers in
several tiers of recognition. Above average reviewers are recognized as Commendable Reviewers. Those in
the top 20% that exhibit diligence, punctuality, and comprehensiveness in their duties are recognized as
Excellent Reviewers. Ultimately, the top 10% who go above and beyond are recognized as Outstanding
Reviewers. Their names are listed as part of this proceedings. We ask that you join us in applauding their
contributions to the community.

Artifact Badging
For the first time, we encourage accepted papers to qualify for the Artifact Available badge by releasing
code, datasets, or similar artifacts through publicly available, archival repositories providing a Document
Object Identifier (DOI). Across the research and ADS tracks, we received 51 artifact applications from 47
papers. After a badge review process led by the artifact badging chairs, 30 artifact applications from 27
papers were approved. This means 10.8% of the accepted papers will feature the Artifact Availability badge
on ACM Digital Library. We look forward to even greater numbers in the future cycles.
