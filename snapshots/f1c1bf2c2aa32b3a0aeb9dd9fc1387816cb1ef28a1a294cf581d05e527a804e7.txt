Welcome Message
WACV 2025
Welcome to Tucson, Arizona, the home of the Winter Conference on Applications of Computer
Vision (WACV) for the first time! As the premier venue for advances in applications of computer
vision, our meeting continues to grow along with the interest in AI and computer vision. For
WACV 2025, we have incorporated new features and made some changes from previous iterations
to accommodate this growth. We hope that you will enjoy the meeting and find time to enjoy
Tucson.
WACV 2025 consists of a three-day main conference program and two days of workshops and
tutorials. Following the WACV tradition, each day of the main conference includes a keynote
presentation, followed by oral and poster presentations. There will also be ample opportunity to
make new acquaintances and socialize with old colleagues during several social events that are
part of the program of WACV this year.
We continue to attract a significant number of submissions, reaching a record 2,458 valid
submissions – around 20% more than WACV 2024. To handle this record number of submissions,
we relied on 199 Area Chairs and 2,472 reviewers. We are especially grateful to the reviewers who
were willing to conduct last-minute emergency reviews, ensuring that all papers received at least
3 reviews. The reviewing process was double-blind and followed best practices to avoid conflicts
of interest. After receiving the reviews, the Area Chairs made decisions in groups of two, so that
each paper was considered by multiple reviewers and multiple Area Chairs.
We continued the WACV tradition of a two-round submission process. By the Round 1 deadline
in mid-July, we received 1406 valid paper submissions. Of these, 167 were accepted directly after
the first round of review, and an additional 710 were invited to be revised and resubmitted to
Round 2, along with a rebuttal. Of the revised papers, 475 were accepted, whereas the rest were
either rejected or withdrawn. The overall acceptance rate for Round 1 was therefore 45.7%.
Authors could also submit new papers directly to Round 2, which had a deadline in early
September. We received 1,052 submissions in Round 2, of which 288 (27.4%) were accepted.
Overall, across both rounds WACV received a total of 2,458 paper submissions and accepted a
total of 930 papers, for an overall acceptance rate of 37.8%. These statistics illustrate how
WACV’s innovative two-round submission process allows authors to incorporate reviewer
feedback to improve their papers (in Round 1), leading to a higher acceptance rate, as well as
receiving quick feedback for Round 2.
The paper review process had two tracks: Applications and Algorithms. While papers in both
tracks were expected to include original, novel, high-quality contributions, papers in the
