13 - 16 July, 2027

Brussels | Belgium

Welcome to ECC27!

It is our great pleasure to welcome you to Brussels for the 25th European Control Conference (ECC). This marks the return of ECC to Brussels for the first time in 30 years! We are honored to host ECC 2027 at the UCLouvain–Woluwé campus, the very same venue as three decades ago, and to offer you the opportunity to engage with academics and industry professionals from around the world.

Control for a Better Life

Immerse yourself in the field of automatic control and connect with academics and industry professionals at the 25th ECC in Brussels, Belgium. In particular:

 * Discover the latest advances in systems and control from internationally renowned experts.

 * Network with colleagues from across Europe and around the world.

 * Enjoy the vibrant city of Brussels at one of the most beautiful times of the year.

Inspired by Brussels’ iconic symbols of good living: Belgian chocolate and flavorful beer, the theme of ECC 2027 is Control for a Better Life. Just as these local delights add joy to daily life, control systems make our world safer, smarter, and more comfortable. Join us to explore how control contributes to a better life for all.
