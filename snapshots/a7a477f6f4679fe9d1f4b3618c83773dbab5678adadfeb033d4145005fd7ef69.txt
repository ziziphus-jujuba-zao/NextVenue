Welcome to 2027 IEEE International Conference on Robotics & Automation (ICRA)!

Save the date for ICRA 2027, May 24th to 28th, 2027 at Coex in Seoul, South Korea!
Keep an eye out for the Call for Papers

The flagship conference of the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS), ICRA brings together the world’s top researchers and industry leaders to share ideas, exchange knowledge, and advance the field of robotics for the benefit of humanity. With a rapidly changing landscape, it has never been more important to attend this leading industry event.

About the Venue

The conference location for ICRA 2027 is Coex – Convention & Exhibition Center in Seoul, the Republic of Korea.

With its modern architecture and 48,157 square meters of flexible event space, Coex stands as one of Asia’s most advanced convention centers.

Visit Seoul

Located in East Asia, the Republic of Korea, population 51 million people, is one of the world’s leading countries in technology and innovation. Korea is well known for its strengths in semiconductors, robotics, artificial intelligence, mobility, biotechnology, and smart infrastructure.
