#AISTATS2026: The titles and abstracts for accepted papers are now *visible in OpenReview*. [AISTATS] International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Statistics 2026 received 2102 submissions, of which 590 (28%) were accepted after careful review and consideration by the reviewers, ACs, and SACs—and ultimately by us Program Chairs (Aaditya Ramdas & Arno Solin). Only 0.5% of all submitted papers were accepted for an Oral presentation; another 2.5% are accepted for a Spotlight presentation. The full papers will be made available before the conference, when the camera-ready deadline has passed. AISTATS 2026 will take place in #Tangier, #Morocco, from May 2 to May 5, 2026, with the last day being a new workshop day. We'd like to invite you to submit your newest work to these workshops.
