Intelligent Control·Autonomous Decision·Smart Future The 39 th Chinese Control and Decision Conference

Changsha, China 14-16 May 2027

----------------------------------------

The 39th Chinese Control and Decision Conference (CCDC 2027) will be held from May 14th to 16th, 2027 in Changsha, China. The Chinese Control and Decision Conference is a major international academic conference in the felds of control, systems, decision theory, and related technologies. It aims to provide a platform for academic exchange among experts, scholars, and engineering technicians from both domestic and international institutions engaged in education and research within these felds. The conference will feature various sessions, including keynote addresses, distinguished lectures, summit forums, thematic workshops, tutorial sessions, oral presentations, and poster sessions. The official language of the conference is English. CCDC 2027 is organized by Northeastern University (NEU), and locally organized by Central South University, Hunan University, National University of Defense Technology. Accepted papers will be submitted for inclusion into IEEE Xplore subject to meeting IEEE Xplore’s scope and quality requirements.

Changsha, also known as Star City, is the capital of Hunan Province and one of China’s first national famous historical and cultural cities. With a unique history of unchanged name and site spanning 3,000 years, it is an important birthplace of Huxiang Culture and a key node city in the Belt and Road Initiative. Boasting a distinctive landscape integrating mountains, rivers, and islets, Changsha blends historical and cultural treasures such as Yuelu Academy and the Mawangdui Han Tombs with modern scientific and technological innovation. It has been successively awarded the titles of UNESCO City of Media Arts, East Asian City of Culture, and has been recognized as one of China’s Happiest Cities for consecutive years.
