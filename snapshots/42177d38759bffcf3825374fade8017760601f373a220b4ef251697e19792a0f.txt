This is the official NeurIPS Paris 2026 registration page, we do not work with any booking agencies.

Registration is managed by Dakini PCO.

Important note for paper authors (core tracks, workshops and competitions):

For main conference papers, one registration spot per accepted paper is guaranteed and reserved for the designated author to present the work in Paris. Designated authors for Paris must register from 7 October to claim their reserved seat. Other co-authors need to register independently as part of the general public registration.

For workshop and competition papers, we have secured a pool of seats which workshop and competition organisers will be able to allocate directly. However, please note that the number of seats in this pool will not cover all papers workshops are planning to accept. We hence encourage workshop-only authors that strongly wish to secure a seat to buy one through the general audience quota.

REGISTRATION CANCELLATION / REFUND POLICY

Registrations canceled by Nov 4, 2026 CET will receive a full refund. Refunds will be issued to your credit card, and may take up to 10 business days to appear on your statement.

Registrations cannot be canceled or refunded after this date. If you cancel your registration when the meeting is sold out, your ticket becomes part of the reserve pool of tickets and you likely will not be able to re-register.

To cancel your registration, please send an email to Registration Management

Registration refunds due to visa issues

If your visa is denied by Nov 4, 2026 CET, you may use the standard cancellation policy detailed above to cancel and refund your registration in full.

From Nov 5, 2026 CET, your registration can be converted to a virtual registration and the difference in cost between the in-person and virtual registration can be refunded if the following conditions are satisfied:

 * you applied for your visa by Oct. 20, 2026;
   AND
 * you are unable to attend the conference in person because your visa was denied or you have not received a response.

Registrations can not be transfered or shared.

INVITATION LETTER

Visa letters of invitation are generated only for the purpose of attending the physical conference. If you need documentation of your participation at the conference for reimbursement, please use your registration receipt as proof of payment and the certificate of attendance you will receive at the end of the conference.

You must be fully registered and have paid the relevant registration fee to receive an invitation letter.

You can apply when registering.

We are not able to provide a letter if you have not registered. No exceptions.

BADGE POLICY

Access to all NeurIPS 2026 in Paris will be allowed only to people wearing a badge.

Badge Replacement Policy

1st Replacement

 * Full Price. You will need to purchase a new registration for the current and remaining conference sessions you wish to attend.
 * Your replacement fee will be refunded after the conference if you return to the registration desk with both badges within 24 hours.

2nd Replacement

 * Full Price. You will need to purchase a new registration for the current and remaining conference sessions you wish to attend.
 * There will be no refund.

Damaged or Updated Badges

 * We will replace for free badges that are damaged, scuffed, or need to be updated due to change of institution.

Please keep track of your badge and make sure you bring it each day.
