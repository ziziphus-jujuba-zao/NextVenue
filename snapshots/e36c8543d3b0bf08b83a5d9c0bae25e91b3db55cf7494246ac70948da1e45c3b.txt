Five papers were accepted at AAAI-25

December 17, 2024 16:33

Five papers were accepted at AAAI 2025, a top conference on artificial intelligence. For more details, please refer to the link below.

[AAAI-25] https://aaai.org/Conferences/AAAI-25/

[Acceptance rate]
There are 3,032 accepted papers from 12,957 submissions, leading to a 23.4 percent acceptance rate.
