* Full Time Student

You must be a full time student in an accredited undergraduate, masters or graduate program or have submitted an accepted paper while you were a full time student.

 * You will be required to upload a jpg of your of your student ID or other documentation that shows that you are a student
 * You will also need to present the documentation when you check in at the registration desk.

Academic

If your expenses are being paid by a degree granting university or non-profit research institute, purchase registration using Academic.

Industrial / Non-Academic

If your expenses are being reimbursed by a corporation or other non-academic institution, please purchase registration with Industrial / Non-Academic pricing.

Virtual Only Pass

The virtual only pass includes virtual-only access to the entire conference's live streams and the ability to interact using Rocket Chat.

Guest Banquet / Reception Tickets for Sydney and Atlanta

The banquet / reception tickets are for guests. A main conference registration includes access to the opening receiption. A workshop registration includes access to the workshop receiption.

 * In Sydney the opening receiption is Monday Dec 7, and the workshop reception is Friday Dec 11.
 * In Atlanta, the opening reception is Wednesday Dec 9, and the workshop reception is Saturday Dec 12.  If you have a registration for both main conference and workshops, a guest banquet will allow your guest to attend both banquets.
 * The Paris banquet / reception tickets will be sold on their registration site.

Registration Cancellation Policy

Registrations canceled before Nov 04, 2026 AOE will receive a full refund. Refunds will be issued to your credit card, and may take up to 10 business days to appear on your statement. Registrations cannot be canceled or refunded after this date. If you cancel your registration when the meeting is sold out, your ticket becomes part of the reserve pool of tickets and you likely will not be able to re-register.

To cancel your registration, follow the instructions here.

Registration refunds due to visa issues

If your visa is denied before Nov 04, 2026 AOE, you may use the standard cancellation policy detailed above to cancel and refund your registration in full.

After Nov 04, 2026 AOE, your full conference registration can be converted to a virtual registration and the difference in cost between the in-person and virtual registration can be refunded if both of the following conditions are satisfied:

 * you applied for your visa by Oct. 20, 2026; and
 * you are unable to attend the conference in person because your visa was denied or you have not received a response.

To request such a refund, you must submit a request using our contact form within one week of the close of the meeting (i.e., by Dec. 20, 2026), and include documentation showing the date you applied for a visa.
