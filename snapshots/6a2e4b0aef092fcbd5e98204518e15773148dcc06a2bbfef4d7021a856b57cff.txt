Annual Meeting & Exhibition

08-13 May 2027
Vancouver, BC, Canada

Accepting Abstracts 01-28 October 2026

Abstract Submission Deadline:

Welcome to Vancouver 2027

The ISMRM & ISMRT Annual Meeting & Exhibition is a global event dedicated exclusively to magnetic resonance in medicine. Bringing together thousands of researchers, clinicians, physicians, engineers, physicists, radiographers, technologists, students, and industry professionals from around the world, the Annual Meeting serves as the flagship gathering of our global MR community.

Guided by the 2027 theme, Development in Every Dimension, the Vancouver meeting will showcase the latest advances in magnetic resonance through scientific presentations, educational sessions, technical exhibits, and networking opportunities that inspire new ideas, foster collaboration, and advance the field of MR.

Information about abstract submissions, registration, housing, the annual meeting program, and important dates will be added to this page as it becomes available. We look forward to welcoming you to Vancouver, BC, Canada, 08-13 May 2027.

Education Dedicated to MR Professionals

Whether your focus is clinical practice, research, technology, or innovation, the ISMRM & ISMRT Annual Meeting offers educational experiences designed for every stage of your magnetic resonance career.

ISMRT – Radiographers & Technologists

 * Three days of focused clinical education designed specifically for MR radiographers and technologists
 * Practical sessions that support hands-on patient care and professional development
 * Connect with MR professionals and colleagues from around the world
 * Early-career professionals encouraged to attend, earn educational credits

ISMRM – Researchers, Clinicians & MR Professionals

 * Six days of comprehensive education covering MR research, clinical practice, and technology
 * Scientific presentations from leading researchers, clinicians, physicians, engineers, physicists, students, and industry professionals
 * Explore groundbreaking research, technical innovations, educational exhibits, and Gold Symposia
 * Connect with our global MR community, discover emerging technologies, and earn educational credits
