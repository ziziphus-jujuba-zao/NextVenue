●

Stressed Conference Publication Model: Options
○ Status Quo: ICRA CEB, IROS CEB, CASE CEB, etc.
■ Pros: familiar
■ Cons: poor review quality, overload, hard to manage
○ Abstract Only Conferences (AdCom in January 2024)- no published
papers with conferences
■ Pros: fewer papers, fewer reviewers, higher quality papers
■ Cons: fewer papers, fewer opportunities for people to publish,
RA-L /T-RO as only option makes bar too high for students
(conferences good training for students), challenge to justify
attending conference/funding
○ New Journal- (RA-L Conference, RAS Proceedings, …) No
review/resubmit cycle, higher bar
■ Pros: All papers are journal papers, one review board for multiple
conferences (special issue for each?) fewer papers reviewed in
total (no evolutionary option)
■ Cons: no evolutionary publication option
○ Discussion:
■ Noted that the root cause of the issues is that we are growing
