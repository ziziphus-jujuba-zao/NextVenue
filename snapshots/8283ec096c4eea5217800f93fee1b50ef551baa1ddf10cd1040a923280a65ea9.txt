 * Registration rates are the same regardless of whether you attend the in-person at the Hilton San Francisco or the OnDemand portion only.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Who do I contact with registration questions or to make a change/update to my existing registration?

A: Please contact the customer service center at [email protected] or call +1-774-247-4023

Q: I’ve registered but am no longer able to attend in person. Can I get a refund?

A: If you’re unable to attend the meeting in person, please note there are no registration refunds as all content will be available on demand after the conference. You must change your participation status in the online Registration Resource Center.

Q: When will the On Demand content be available?

A: OnDemand content will be available to all registrants on December 21. Access instructions will be provided via email at that time.

Q: How do I get my badge?

A: Onsite registration will be in the Plaza A Ballroom on the Lobby Level of Hilton San Francisco Union Square:

 * Saturday, December 12 12:00 pm – 6:00 pm (Tutorial registrants only from 12:00 pm – 3:30pm. All others may pick up badges at 3:30 pm)
 * Sunday, December 13 8:00 am – 5:00 pm
 * Monday, December 14 8:00 am – 5:00 pm
 * Tuesday, December 15 8:30 am – 5:00 pm
 * Wednesday, December 16 8:30 am – 3:00 pm

Q: I am an international registrant, how do I obtain my visa letter?

A: You will receive a confirmation email after registering for the conference. Please scroll down to the section titled Registration Resource Center and click on the link to access your registration portal where you’ll find the visa letter. Note: You must be registered with an address outside of the U.S. to obtain the visa letter.

Q: I am an IEEE member but why am I unable to register at the member rates?

A: Make sure you’re using the same email and password on file with IEEE Membership. Should you have further issues please contact the IEDM registration service center at [email protected] or call +1 774-247-4023.

Q: Why am I having trouble getting to the registration website?

A: Clear your cache and/or open new incognito window. Should you have further issues please contact the IEDM registration service center at [email protected] or call +1 774-247-4023.
