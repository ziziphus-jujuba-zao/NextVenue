Important Dates and Submission Steps

 * May 26, 2026: Submission Site Open.
 * July 19, 2026 23:59 PDT: Submission Deadline EXTENDED
 * August 3, 2026: Late News Paper Submission Open.
 * August 18, 2026 23:59 PDT: Late News Paper Submission Closes.
 * September 9: Paper Acceptance/Rejection Notifications.

Step 1: Submit your file for review only after reading the following information, including instructions on the Preparation of Papers page.

Step 2: Upload the source file for your paper to IEEE PDF Express and approve the Xplore compliant PDF created by that site. For further details, review the Preparation of Papers and follow the instructions under How to Create an IEEE Xplore Compliant PDF.

Step 3: The author should upload the PDF created from the PDF Express site to the IEDM Submission site.

IMPORTANT: Successfully uploading your paper does not indicate your paper fulfills the page limits or formatting requirements. This will only be verified by IEDM at a later stage and submissions that are found in violation of those requirements will be rejected from consideration. It is the author’s responsibility to fully follow the Preparation of Papers.

Accepted papers will be published AS IS. No changes are permitted after acceptance, including author list and funding acknowledements, etc. All accepted authors are also required to provide the following for the OnDemand:
 * 20-minute Video Presentation
 * PDF Slide Presentation on IEDM Template

Submission Guidelines

Paper Guidelines

In addition to the guidelines below, please refer to the Pre-Submission Checklist to avoid potential issues and delays with reviewing your paper.

Paper Requirements:

Papers must be a 4-page camera-ready paper (2 pages of text and 2 pages of figures).

Clearly State:

 * Purpose of work
 * Manner and degree to which it advances the art accompanied with proper references
 * Specific new results that have been obtained with clear experimental conditions and their significance
 * Degree to which the paper deals with these issues will strongly affect whether the paper is accepted
 * Title of paper
 * Name, affiliation, city, state and country, email of the first author
 * Up to two pages of text and up to two additional pages of figures and drawings (no text, captions only) in 8-1/2” x 11” format describing the planned 20-minute paper and emphasizing the findings. The font size for the body of the text must be at least 10 point.
 * Excessive photo reduction of figures and poor legibility will negatively impact acceptance
 * Papers with more than 2 pages of text or 2 pages of figures shall be grounds for immediate rejection.
 * Avoid special international fonts
 * Page margins given in the template document will be enforced prior to submission

The most common cause of rejection:

 * Lack of specific results.
 * Only work that has not been previously published at the time of the conference will be considered.
 * Paper acceptance will be based solely on the information provided on the four-page paper submitted. Promises of upcoming results will be ignored. All submissions will be checked for plagiarism.

Submission Details

In addition to the information below, please refer to the Pre-Submission Details to avoid potential issues and delays with your submission.

Only electronic submissions received by the deadline via the Mira site will be reviewed. Submissions sent via email or mailed hard copies will not be reviewed.

The following information MUST be entered in Mira:

 * Paper Title, Author List and Summary – NO changes permitted after July 16, 2026.
 * Identification as a regular “contributed” paper or a “student paper”
 * Suggested area/category (see Call for Papers Overview)
 * 150-word abstract (Do not include as a separate page with your submission)
 * Agree to Pre-Publicity and IEDM Policies
 * Convert paper to PDF Express format and review to ensure it’s free of errors/missing information. Upload that paper to Mira. No changes can be made to pdf paper after completing the PDF Express conversion.

REQUIRED: Prior to submission, the paper must be submitted to the IEEE PDF Express site to create the Xplore compliant PDF.

Once you have reviewed the Xplore compliant PDF for accuracy, upload that file to Mira. Papers not converted to the Xplore compliant PDF will be rejected.

 * Submitted paper must be PDF Express compliant
 * Paper should match the author list, title and summary as noted in the electronic submission
 * The author must review the PDF Express version to ensure it’s free of formatting issues and missing information.

Successfully uploading your paper does not indicate your paper fulfills the page limits or formatting requirements. This will only be verified by IEDM at a later stage and submissions that are found in violation of those requirements will be rejected from consideration.

Paper Acceptance

Acceptance Details

If accepted, the 4-page paper will be used for publication as submitted. No changes are allowed once the paper has been submitted for review.

If accepted, authors are required to transfer their copyrights in a final submission stage, which will be in September after the paper selection meeting.

Acceptance Notification

 * Authors of accepted papers will be notified by September 9. The accepted paper will be published as-is in the IEDM Technical Digest. Publication in the digest in no way precludes later publication of a fuller account of the work in another journal, but NO PUBLICATION is acceptable before the conference. The paper must be presented at the conference by one of the listed authors.
 * Your paper has been accepted based on several conditions:
 * All authors must present in-person.
 * The paper will not be published in its entirety prior to the conference.
 * You must remain for 10 to 15 minutes after the session to address questions from the audience.
