Conference Venues

All ACM MM’25 conference venues are located within a short walking distance of one another in Dublin city centre. We have selected these venues for their central locations, excellent transport connections, and proximity to Dublin’s vibrant social and cultural quarter.

Dublin Royal Convention Centre / Radisson

The main ACM MM’25 conference venue is the Dublin Royal Convention Centre, located in the heart of Dublin’s cultural quarter overlooking Dublin Castle. The venue is centrally located and easily accessible via Dublin’s public transport network.

Please note that the conference centre occupies two sides of the building. In the conference programme, these are identified as Royal CC (Convention Centre) and Radisson. The Radisson side is connected to the hotel, where many conference attendees will be staying.

Please check the venue listed for your session carefully and make sure you proceed to the correct side of the building.

Address:
One Le Pole Square, Ship Street Great, Dublin, D08 E6PD

Hyatt Centric Liberties

A number of ACM MM’25 sessions will take place at the nearby Hyatt Centric Liberties, approximately an 8-minute walk from the main conference venue.

The Hyatt has its own dedicated conference facilities and will also feature a satellite registration desk. Refreshments and lunch will be available at the Hyatt for attendees whose sessions are held there.

Address:
Dean St, The Liberties, Dublin, D08 W3X7

Guinness Storehouse — Gala Dinner

The ACM MM’25 social evening will take place at the Guinness Storehouse, with the conference hosting a private opening of this world-famous Dublin attraction.

The Guinness Storehouse is located a short walk from the main conference venue, making it convenient for attendees to travel between the conference and evening event.

Address:
St. James’s Gate, Dublin 8, D08 VF8H
