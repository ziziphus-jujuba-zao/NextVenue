About

Topics

Transportation

Convened by the Transportation Research Board (TRB), the 2027 TRB Annual Meeting will be held January 10–14, 2027, at the Walter E. Washington Convention Center and Marriott Marquis in Washington, D.C.

Join thousands of transportation leaders, innovators, and decision-makers from around the globe for one of the industry’s most influential gatherings. From cutting-edge research to real-world solutions, the Annual Meeting delivers an unmatched program spanning every mode of transportation—designed to spark ideas, foster collaboration, and shape the future of mobility.

Engage in thought-provoking sessions and hands-on workshops tackling the most pressing challenges facing the field today. Whether you’re a policymaker, practitioner, researcher, or industry professional, this is where connections are made, insights are shared, and the next generation of transportation solutions takes shape.

About TRB

TRB is a leading organization in transportation research and provides valuable resources and expertise to transportation professionals and policymakers worldwide. As part of the National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine, the Transportation Research Board (TRB) mobilizes expertise, experience, and knowledge to anticipate and solve complex transportation-related challenges.

Thomas B. Deen Awardee Announced

Thomas B. Deen Awardee Announced

Thomas B. Deen Distinguished Lecture

Kay Fitzpatrick, senior research fellow at the Texas A&M Transportation Institute, is the 2027 recipient of the Thomas B. Deen Distinguished Lectureship. Throughout her career, Fitzpatrick has advanced the fields of roadway geometric design, pedestrian traffic control devices, and transportation safety through research that has shaped transportation practice across the United States. Fitzpatrick will deliver her lecture titled Addressing Roadway Safety, One Step at a Time on Monday, January 11. Read the full announcement.

Frank Turner Medal Awardee Announced

Frank Turner Medal Awardee Announced

Frank Turner Medal

Anne Aylward is the is the recipient of the 2027 Frank Turner Medal for Lifetime Achievement in Transportation. As the former director of the U.S. DOT Volpe National Transportation Systems Center and throughout her career, Aylward has exemplified leadership, public service, and enduring advancements to transportation for the public good. Learn more about her work. The award will be presented during the Chair’s Plenary Session on Wednesday, January 13.

Skinner Management Awardee Announced

Skinner Management Awardee Announced

Skinner Management Award

Amy Stearns is the 2026 recipient of the Robert E. Skinner, Jr. Distinguished Transportation Research Management Award. Formerly with the U.S. Department of Transportation, she is recognized for more than three decades of effective leadership of the University Transportation Centers Program, advancing transportation research, education, technology transfer, and partnerships. The award will be presented during the Chair’s Plenary Session on Wednesday, January 13.
