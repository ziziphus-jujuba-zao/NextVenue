Getting to Chicago

Chicago is easily accessible by air, car, train, and other means of transportation.

✈️ By Air

Chicago O'Hare International (ORD)

Major international hub with direct flights worldwide

Travel time: 30-45 minutes to McCormick Place via taxi/Uber

🚗 By Car

Interstate Access

I-90 and I-94 provide direct access to downtown Chicago

Parking: On-site at McCormick Place ($20-30/day)

🚆 By Train

Amtrak - Union Station

Scenic route with connections from major East Coast cities

To venue: Short taxi/CTA ride (5-10 minutes)

🚌 Public Transit

CTA Red Line

Museum Campus/11th St station near McCormick Place

Cost: $2.50 single / $31 3-day pass

Getting Around Chicago

 * CTA (Chicago Transit Authority): Buses, trains, elevated rail - safe and affordable
 * Taxi & Rideshare: Uber, Lyft widely available throughout the city
 * Divvy Bikes: Bike-sharing program for short trips
 * Walking: Downtown is very walkable with good pedestrian infrastructure

Travel Tips

 * Book flights 6-8 weeks in advance for better rates
 * Mid-week flights are typically cheaper than weekends
 * Allow extra travel time for Chicago traffic during rush hours
 * Download Citymapper app for real-time transit navigation

Frequently Asked Questions

When is JMM 2027?

JMM 2027 takes place January 12-15, 2027 at McCormick Place in Chicago, Illinois.

What is the registration fee?

Registration fees vary by category. Member early bird pricing starts at $179. Student rates are available at $59. Visit the registration page for complete details and deadlines.

Can I present at JMM?

Yes! JMM accepts contributed papers, posters, and special session proposals. Call for proposals typically opens in spring. Check the website for current deadlines.

Is there student support?

Yes. Student registration is discounted, and some scholarships are available for student travel. Graduate students can apply for presentation opportunities.

Will there be online attendance?

Yes, JMM 2027 will offer virtual attendance options for those unable to attend in person. More details coming soon.

How do I book a hotel with the JMM rate?

Use the official JMM housing portal to book hotels in the negotiated block. You must book by December 31, 2026 to receive the special rates.

What accessibility accommodations are available?

McCormick Place is fully accessible. If you need specific accommodations beyond standard accessibility, please contact [email protected] at least 30 days in advance.

When will the mobile app be available?

The JMM 2027 mobile app will be available for download in December 2026. It will feature the complete program schedule, speaker directory, venue maps, and real-time updates.

About JMM

The Joint Mathematics Meetings, held annually since 1916, represent the largest gathering of mathematicians in the world. The meetings are held for the benefit of all of its participants ? speakers, students, instructors, department chairs, exhibitors, and visitors.
