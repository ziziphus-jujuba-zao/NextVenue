Future ASSA Annual Meetings

The AEA, in conjunction with 66 associations in related disciplines, holds a three-day meeting each January to present papers on general economic subjects. Over 550 scholarly sessions are held. In 2026, 5,113 registered.

SCHEDULE OF FUTURE MEETINGS:

January 3-5, 2027 (Sunday, Monday & Tuesday)
Washington, DC – Marriott Marquis

January 7-9, 2028 (Friday, Saturday & Sunday)
Atlanta, GA – Atlanta Marriott Marquis

January 5-7, 2029 (Friday, Saturday & Sunday)
San Diego, CA – San Diego Marriott Marquis and Marina
