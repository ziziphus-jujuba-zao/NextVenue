49th Annual International Conference of the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society

International Convention Centre (ICC)
Suntec Singapore Convention & Exhibition Centre, Singapore •
11 – 15 July 2027

Welcome to IEEE EMBC 2027

On behalf of the Organizing Committee, it is our great pleasure to welcome you to the 49th Annual International Conference of the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society (EMBC 2027), taking place from 11–15 July 2027 in Singapore.

EMBC is the flagship conference of the IEEE Engineering Medicine and Biology Society and brings together researchers, clinicians, engineers, industry leaders, entrepreneurs, and students from around the world to share the latest advances in biomedical engineering, healthcare technologies, and life sciences.

As one of Asia’s leading hubs for healthcare innovation, biomedical research, and digital health, Singapore provides an ideal setting for EMBC 2027. The conference will serve as a global platform for interdisciplinary collaboration and knowledge exchange, showcasing cutting-edge developments that are transforming healthcare and improving lives worldwide.

EMBC 2027 will feature an exciting technical program comprising plenary and keynote lectures by internationally renowned experts, scientific and technical sessions, workshops, tutorials, special sessions, industry exhibits, and networking opportunities. We look forward to fostering meaningful discussions and collaborations that will shape the future of medicine, biology, and healthcare technologies.

Beyond the conference, we encourage you to explore Singapore’s vibrant multicultural heritage, world-class attractions, exceptional culinary scene, and thriving innovation ecosystem.

We warmly invite you to join us in Singapore for an inspiring and memorable EMBC 2027. We look forward to welcoming you and sharing this exciting journey together.

Sincerely,
Liang Zhong,
General Chair, IEEE EMBC 2027

Rosa So, May Wang, Paul Sajda
General Co-Chairs, IEEE EMBC 2027

Conference Theme

Translating Healthcare Innovation: Bridging Labs with Industries

Building on a strong legacy of scientific excellence, the 2027 conference will focus on the theme “Translating Healthcare Innovation: Bridging Labs with Industries” highlighting how breakthroughs in engineering, AI, and biomedical science can be transformed into scalable solutions that benefit patients, providers, and health systems. At this world’s largest international biomedical engineering conference, a broad spectrum of tracks will cover cutting-edge research in biomedical devices, digital health, AI and data science, imaging, biosensors, robotics, and health informatics, alongside sessions dedicated to translation, regulation, and entrepreneurship.

Under this theme, the conference will bring together researchers, engineers, clinicians, policymakers, and industry partners to accelerate the journey from prototype to practice. We particularly welcome contributions that showcase successful academic–industry collaborations, translational pipelines, real-world deployments, and regulatory or reimbursement pathways for innovative technologies. In addition to distinguished keynotes, the program will feature workshops, mini-symposia, special sessions, oral and poster presentations, industry showcases, and events tailored for students, early-career investigators, and startups. We invite you to share your latest advances and join a global community committed to turning lab discoveries into impactful healthcare innovations.
