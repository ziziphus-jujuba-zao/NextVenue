About CICTP 2027

The 27th COTA International Conference of Transportation Professionals (CICTP 2027) will be held from July 16 to 18, 2027, in Beijing, China, and hosted by Tsinghua University. As the host institution, Tsinghua University warmly welcomes researchers, practitioners, policymakers, and industry leaders worldwide to share their latest research achievements, innovative technologies, and practical experiences.

At the current stage, comprehensive three-dimensional transportation has become an important strategic direction in China’s 15th Five-Year Plan. With the transformation of China’s transportation development from large-scale infrastructure construction toward system upgrading and quality enhancement, improving the intelligence and connectivity of integrated transportation systems has become increasingly important. In particular, leveraging advanced technologies such as artificial intelligence to promote the paradigm transformation of comprehensive three-dimensional transportation development is of great practical significance for advancing future transportation systems.

With the theme “Connected, Integrated, Upgraded: Paradigm Shift in Comprehensive Three-Dimensional Transportation,” CICTP 2027 aims to provide a high-level platform for academic exchange and collaboration, bringing together experts from transportation and related fields to explore emerging theories, innovative technologies, and practical applications that promote intelligent, integrated, and high-quality transportation development.

Established in 1996, Chinese Overseas Transportation Association (COTA, formerly NACOTA) is a non-profit professional organization that aims at promoting and facilitating communications among transportation professionals worldwide and their counterparts in China. COTA has more than 1000 members and friends worldwide and the number is growing.

As the flagship event of COTA, COTA International Conference of Transportation Professionals (CICTP) is a leading international academic conference that serves as the premier gathering of transportation professionals worldwide who are interested in contributing to or gaining a profound understanding of transportation development in China.
