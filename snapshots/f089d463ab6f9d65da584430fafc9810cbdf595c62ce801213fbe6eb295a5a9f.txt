Important Dates

Please note that the meeting dates are earlier than usual; therefore, deadlines may fall earlier in the year. Anticipate your participation on time!

 * Mid-June: Session submission opens.
 * 15 August: Session submission deadline
 * 25 August: Training course proposals open
 * Early September: Grant applications open
 * Early September: Volunteer applications open
 * End of September: Abstract submissions open
 * 14 October: Training course proposal deadline
 * 5 November: Volunteer application deadline
 * 10 November: Abstract submission deadline
 * 17 November: Student grants appliaction deadline

Society of Environmental Toxicology and Chemistry

SETAC is dedicated to advancing environmental science and science-informed decision-making through collaboration, communication, education and leadership. We fulfill that purpose through events, publications, awards and education programs.
