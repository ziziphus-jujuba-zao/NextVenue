Registration FAQ

R1. I have more than one accepted main conference paper. Do I need to pay registration fee for each one?

No. One regular delegate registration fee OR student with paper fee covers multiple papers.

R2. I am a student with an accepted main conference paper. Do I have to pay regular delegate registration fee for my paper to appear in the Proceedings?

Yes. You should choose the student with Paper option though so you can enjoy the student reception as well. You can apply for a grant to defer the cost, however: https://2026.ijcai.org/call-for-grant-applications/

R3. I cannot attend the conference and I already paid my registration fee. Can I transfer my registration to a colleague who will attend?

No. Registration is not transferable. You have to attend and present the paper in-person.

R4. I am a PPI beneficiary but cannot attend. Can I transfer the benefit to one of my colleagues/student?

No, PPI benefits are non-transferable as aimed at recognizing personal contribution.

R5. How can I receive an invitation letter for oral presentation at the conference?

The letter will be sent to you automatically with registration confirmation. Note that this is NOT a visa letter and do not fill visa letter form for that purpose!

R6. How can I receive a letter of invitation for Schengen Visa?

By registering. You will then be able to tick a box and fill out the form that will generate an invitation letter once the registration is completed.
Bear in mind that you can fill the form only once!

R7. Can I have a letter of invitation for a visa before I register?

No. Letters are only issued to registrants who have already paid their fees.

R8. I am a student and my proof of student status fails verification. Why?

Please read carefully the letter of rejection and read General Information 1.1. Students section under Registration/Register referring to proof of student status before you attempt to re-submit.

R9. I am an IJCAI or EurAI member. Do I get a discount?

Yes, each member pays Members’ Fee which is USD 50 less than non-member fee. Discount is applicable to all main conference registrations but not to PPI benefits.

R10. I am neither IJCAI nor EurAI member. Can I become a member now?

Yes, you can become an IJCAI member for an additional discounted fee of USD 100 for 3 years (instead of USD 150) or USD 50 for 3 years (instead of USD 75) as a student. EurAI membership is handled through EurAI directly. Obtaining membership now qualifies for the next 3 years of discounts on registration fees.

R11. I am a PPI beneficiary and EurAI/IJCAI member, why is not USD 50 discount applied to my grand total registration fee on top of my PPI discount?

PPI discount cannot be combined with membership discounts, and is non-transferable, as it is intended to recognize your personal contribution during the reviewing process.

R12 . I am a journalist. How do I register for a press pass?

Please write an email to registration@ijcai.org and provide your family name, first name and exact affiliation.

R13 . I need to cancel my workshop only registration. Do I still have to pay U$ 150 fee?

Yes, U$ 150 processing fee applies to any and all cancellations.

R14 . I want to buy an extra IJCAI opening reception and banquet ticket. Where can I do that?

Through the registration system

R15 . When will my regular talk be?

Regular talks will be scheduled between August 18 and August 21, 2026. The schedule will be available in July. If you have hard constraints, please write to the Program Chair: pcchair@2026.ijcai.org

R16 . We would like to register a group of students/scientific staff members. Is that possible?

No. Each person must register personally and separately for legal reasons

R17 . I have a registration related question, who do I contact?

registration@ijcai.org

R18 .I am from India and my company requires a notice from IJCAI about not having a PE in India.

IJCAI has no PE in India and will not issue other statements to this effect.
