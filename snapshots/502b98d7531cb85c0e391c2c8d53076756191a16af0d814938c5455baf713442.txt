    2027 AERA Annual Meeting Notifications to be Sent November 6
    The 2027 AERA Annual Meeting theme—“Transcending Borders and
    Boundaries in Education Research: Toward a Beloved Global
    Community”—“responds to the challenge of the moment while
    serving as an invitation.” The 2027 Annual Meeting will take
    place in Toronto, Canada, April 14-18, 2027. The Call for
    Submissions is now closed. Notifications will be sent
    November 6.

    2026 Annual Meeting Wraps Up in Los Angeles
    The 2026 AERA Annual Meeting was in Los Angeles, April 8-12,
    2026. Recordings from major lectures and sessions will be
    forthcoming. Watch the 2026 wrap up video.

    Annual Meeting Site Selection Process
    Site selection for the AERA Annual Meeting, which gathers
    more than 15,000 attendees each year, is undertaken with
    thorough consideration of multiple factors, including, but
    not limited to, geographic and cultural diversity, costs for
    attendees, available meeting space, and social justice
    record. Read more
