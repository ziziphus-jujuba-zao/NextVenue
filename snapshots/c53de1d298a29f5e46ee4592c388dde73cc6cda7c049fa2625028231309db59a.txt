ASSA 2027 Annual Meeting

Washington DC January 03 – 05

The 2027 Annual Meeting will be held on January 3–5, 2027 (Sunday, Monday, Tuesday) in Washington, DC. The headquarters hotel is the Marriott Marquis Washington, DC.

The AEA submission portal is closed. The deadline for individual paper proposals was April 1, 2026, and the deadline for complete session proposals was April 15, 2026.

To host an event at the 2027 ASSA Annual Meeting in Washington, DC, please fill out the event form.
Get the most out of ASSA 2027!

 * The AEA Recent Development Series incorporates continuing education into the annual meeting program.
 * AEA lightning round sessions are back for ASSA 2027!
 * Sign up for the AEA Mentoring and Networking Luncheon when you register.
 * Don't miss the featured AEA lectures!
