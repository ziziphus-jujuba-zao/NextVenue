About ACM Multimedia

Since its launch in 1993, ACM Multimedia (ACM MM) has been the premier international conference for multimedia research, innovation, and applications. It brings together researchers, practitioners, artists, and industry leaders to present and discuss advances in multimedia systems, content understanding, user experience, and emerging applications.

The conference program is organized around a broad set of topics that reflect both foundational research areas and emerging directions in multimedia. The current topics of interest, approved by the Steering Committee, include (in alphabetical order):

 * Art and Culture
 * Data Systems Management and Indexing
 * Embodied and Immersive Multimedia
 * Emotional and Social Signals
 * Interactions and Quality of Experience
 * Multimedia Applications
 * Multimedia Generative and Foundation Models
 * Multimedia Interpretability and Explainability
 * Multimedia and Language
 * Multimedia Search and Recommendation
 * Multimedia Reasoning
 * Multimodal Fusion
 * New Forms of Media Content
 * Responsible Multimedia
 * Summarization, Analytics, and Storytelling
 * Transport and Delivery

A detailed description of these topics is available in the ACM Multimedia Topics of Interest document.

The conference also features technical sessions, tutorials, panels, and an interactive art exhibit, fostering dialogue between scientific innovation and creative exploration in art and cultural heritage.
